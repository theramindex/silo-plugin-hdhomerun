# HDHomeRun Design Context

## Users

Self-hosted Silo viewers browse local live television and programme guides; administrators connect tuners and emulators. They often use the interface from a couch, tablet, or desktop and need clear status, large targets, and predictable back navigation.

## Brand And Tone

HDHomeRun should feel like a native part of Silo: calm, cinematic, precise, and media-forward without becoming a separate streaming brand. Operational screens stay compact and legible; viewing surfaces can use real programme artwork when it is available.

## Visual Direction

- Dark Silo surfaces with restrained borders and no decorative gradients or fake atmospheric artwork.
- Real media and programme information are the strongest signals. Use disciplined typography when artwork is unavailable.
- Use a compact list for channels and endpoint health; reserve a distinct surface for the player.
- Keep corner radii at 8px or below and controls at least 44px on touch layouts.
- Prefer dense, scannable rails and grids over oversized empty hero treatments.
